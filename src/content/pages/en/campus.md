Our campus is in Brampton, at 1 Bartley Bull Pkwy. We have twelve classrooms, each with a maximum of 8 students. It is not that the rooms cannot hold more: beyond that, someone would stop getting a turn to speak in every class.

## Classrooms

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Number | 12 classrooms |
| Capacity | Up to 8 students (small group); two rooms can be combined for 16 people at talks |
| Furniture | Movable tables and chairs, easily arranged in a discussion circle or in pairs |
| Whiteboards | One large whiteboard per room and one mobile whiteboard |
| Screen | Projector or 65-inch TV, for listening materials and timing mock exams |
| Soundproofing | Classrooms are soundproofed from each other |
| Air conditioning | Yes |

The tables move because half of a language class is small-group practice, which does not work in a room with fixed rows.

## Learning resources

- **Textbook:** printed handouts common to each level, written to the CEFR, plus an online exercise bank valid for one year ($84 per level, included in every bundle).
- **Library corner:** about 120 books.

## Study and waiting areas

- **Study area:** 10 seats, open outside class hours; students can stay to do homework.
- **Free Wi-Fi** throughout the campus.
- **Children’s waiting area:** children can wait there while a parent is in class (no supervision is provided).

## Online classes

Online and in-person classes have the same teacher, the same handouts and the same speaking time, at the same price. We do not replace the class with a recording.

- **Platform:** Zoom.
- **Equipment:** each classroom has a camera and an omnidirectional microphone, so online students can hear the whole class.
- **Replays:** every class is recorded and kept for 30 days.
- **Requirement:** camera on. If we cannot see your mouth, we cannot correct your pronunciation.

## Mock-exam room

One room is set aside for mock exams, separate from the teaching area, with no classes scheduled in it on mock-exam days.

- 8 closed-back headphones, with disposable ear covers.
- 1 invigilator per session.
- Listening is played once.
- Scratch paper is handed out and collected; phones go in a bag.
- Results are given the same day.

See the [Mock exams](/mock-exams) page.

## Accessibility and safety

- Fire exits are marked and there is an emergency phone.
- Classroom doors do not lock: during children’s classes, parents can walk in at any time.
