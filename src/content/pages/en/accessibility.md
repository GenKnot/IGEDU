title: "Accessibility statement"
description: "Our commitment to the accessibility of igedu.ca and how to report a barrier."
---

Global French Education Inc. is committed to making igedu.ca usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

## What we do

- Keyboard navigation, a skip link to the main content, visible focus indicators.
- A consistent heading structure and page landmarks (`main`, `nav`, `footer`).
- Colour contrast checked for body text.
- Forms with labels and readable error messages.
- The site is available in French and English.

## Known limits

We test the site ourselves and have not had an external audit: some issues may remain. Third-party sites (for example the exam bodies’ websites) are the responsibility of their publishers.

## Report a barrier

Write to [info@igedu.ca](mailto:info@igedu.ca) describing the page and the problem. We reply within a reasonable time and, where needed, offer another way to get the information (by email or phone).

This statement will be updated as the site evolves.
