Each bundle combines single courses from the [catalogue](/catalogue) into a complete path. For each one you will find who it is for, the stages, what is included and the price. Prices are explained on the [Prices](/prices) page.

- Prices are in Canadian dollars, before tax: 13% HST is added at checkout.
- The textbook is included in every bundle.
- A bundle price can be paid in monthly instalments with no fee.
- For 3 months after a bundle ends, you can ask our teachers questions for free (bundles only).
- The placement test is free (40 minutes): [book yours](/contact).

## The seven bundles

- [**Beginner foundation, A0 to A2**](/bundles/beginner) : 120 hours · about 8 months · 4 hours per week · $3,612 (about $451 per month)

- [**Full-time intensive, 300 hours in 12 weeks**](/bundles/intensive) : 300 hours · 12 weeks · 25 hours per week · $9,450

- [**Full year for youth**](/bundles/teens) : 136 hours · about 12 months · $4,060

- [**Annual corporate program**](/bundles/corporate) : 200–400 hours per year · reviewed every quarter · $35/hour × number of participants, 20% off from 200 hours, 25% off from 400 hours
