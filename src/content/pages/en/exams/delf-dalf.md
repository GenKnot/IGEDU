**DELF** (B1, B2) and **DALF** (C1) are diplomas from France Éducation international. They never expire, but **IRCC does not accept them for immigration**: for Express Entry points you need [TEF Canada](/exams/tef-canada) or [TCF Canada](/exams/tcf-canada).

## The essentials

| | |
|---|---|
| Use | Study, professional recognition, permanent proof of level |
| Sections | All four: listening, reading, writing, speaking |
| Scoring | Out of 100, 50 to pass, at least 5/25 on each section (DELF) |
| Length | DELF B2: about 2 h 50; DALF: see France Éducation international |
| Results | Within 2 months |
| Validity | For life |

## How we prepare you

- [DELF B1](/exam-prep): 36 hours, 6 modules
- [DELF B2](/exam-prep): 48 hours, 8 modules, with oral presentation and debate
- [DALF C1](/exam-prep): 48 hours, 8 modules, with synthesis and oral presentation

Each course ends with two full simulations marked point by point.

## Registration

Session dates, registration fees and test centres are set by the official body and change: check its website before you register. We are **not an accredited test centre**; we help you register and guide you to a partner centre.
