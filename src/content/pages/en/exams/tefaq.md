**TEFAQ** is the French test used for immigration to Quebec. It is run by the CCI Paris Île-de-France.

## The essentials

| | |
|---|---|
| Use | Immigration to Quebec |
| Required sections | Listening and speaking |
| Optional sections | Reading and writing |
| Length | About 1 hour |
| Results | About 2 to 3 weeks |
| Validity | 2 years |

If your plan is federal, this is not the right test: see [TEF Canada](/exams/tef-canada) or [TCF Canada](/exams/tcf-canada). To compare them: [Exams](/exams).

## How we prepare you

Our [TEFAQ course](/exam-prep) is 36 hours, 60% of it on speaking, with a module on Quebec French (accents, local expressions) and an optional reading and writing module. Two full speaking simulations with feedback.

## Registration

Session dates, registration fees and test centres are set by the official body and change: check its website before you register. We are **not an accredited test centre**; we help you register and guide you to a partner centre.
