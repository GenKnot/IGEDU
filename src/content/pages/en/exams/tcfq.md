**TCFQ** is the France Éducation international test used for immigration to Quebec.

## The essentials

| | |
|---|---|
| Use | Immigration to Quebec |
| Required sections | Listening and speaking |
| Optional sections | Reading and writing |
| Results | See the France Éducation international website |
| Validity | 2 years |

If your plan is federal, see [TCF Canada](/exams/tcf-canada). To compare all the tests: [Exams](/exams).

## How we prepare you

Our [TCFQ course](/exam-prep) is 36 hours, 60% of it on speaking, with a module on Quebec French and an optional reading and writing module.

## Registration

Session dates, registration fees and test centres are set by the official body and change: check its website before you register. We are **not an accredited test centre**; we help you register and guide you to a partner centre.
