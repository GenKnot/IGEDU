The hardest step in learning a language is walking into the classroom.

So we split that step into two: a fixed **open day** (free) and a **trial lesson** you can book at any time ($35, credited toward tuition if you enrol).

## Two ways in

| | Open day | Paid trial lesson |
|---|---|---|
| When | First Saturday of each quarter | Weekdays, at your convenience; book 48 hours ahead |
| Length | 90 minutes | 30 minutes |
| What it includes | Campus tour, 30-minute trial lesson and a free placement test | You sit in on the first 30 minutes of a real class |
| Cost | Free | $35 (credited toward tuition if you enrol) |
| Places | Up to 12 people per session | 1 person |
| Best for | Still comparing options | You have decided to learn and want to check the teacher and the pace |

## What you will see

**What you can see:**

- The start of a real class, not a show lesson prepared for visitors.
- How the teacher handles a student who does not understand, which matters more than how well they lecture.
- The level of the other students, which decides whether you would be held back or left behind.
- The classrooms and their soundproofing as they really are.

**What you will not see:**

- The full course structure, which takes several weeks in a row to see.
- Your actual level, which needs a placement test.
- The teacher marking homework or going over a mock exam.

**We do not stage show lessons.** The class you sit in on is the one current students are taking at that moment.

## How a trial lesson works

| Step | Length | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Book | | Through the form below or by email: tell us the level you are interested in and when you can come |
| 2. Confirmation | Within 24 hours | We reply with a specific class and time |
| 3. Arrive | 10 minutes early | Sign in at reception and get a seat |
| 4. Sit in | 30 minutes | You sit at the back of the room and do not take part |
| 5. Talk | 15 minutes | With the teacher or staff: does what you saw match what you need? |
| 6. Optional placement test | 40 minutes | Free, can be booked on the spot |

**Step 5 is the key one.** After the class we do not start selling. We ask whether you can keep up with this pace. If not, we offer a different class to observe.

## FAQ

**What should I bring?**
ID for sign-in at reception. No textbook needed.

**Can I bring my children?**
Yes, there is a children’s waiting area. We do not provide supervision, though.

**Do I have to enrol after the trial lesson?**
No. If you do enrol, the $35 is credited toward your tuition.

**Can I choose the teacher?**
Yes. When you book, tell us the focus (exam, placement, teens) and we will arrange a suitable teacher.

**Can I try a lesson online?**
Yes. The online trial is also 30 minutes; you listen in with your microphone muted.

## Where to start

Want to come and see? Pick a time: we will show you around, and you can take a placement test while you are here. No need to enrol first.
