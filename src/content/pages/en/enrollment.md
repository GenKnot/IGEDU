Six steps, from enquiry to your first class. It usually takes **5 to 10 days**.

If you are short on time, steps 2 and 3 can be done on the same day.

## The six steps

| # | Step | What you do | What we do | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enquiry | Tell us your goal and availability | Give initial advice, schedule a placement test | Same day |
| 2 | Placement test | On site or online, 40 minutes | Give your level on the spot, plus written advice within 24 hours | 1 day |
| 3 | Choosing a course | Choose a path, class and time slot | Offer available sessions and a quote | 1 day |
| 4 | Registration | Fill in the registration form, accept the [terms](/terms) | Send confirmation and payment instructions | Same day |
| 5 | Payment | Pay in full or in instalments | Issue a receipt within 5 business days | 1 day |
| 6 | Before classes start | Receive your account and pre-course instructions | Open the resource library, set up the class group, tell you when your first class is | 2 days before start |

## Details of each step

### Step 2: placement test

- **Format:** 40 minutes in all: a 30-minute written test (listening, reading, grammar) plus a 10-minute speaking test
- **Where:** on site, or online (video and screen sharing)
- **Result:** verbal feedback on the spot, written advice within 24 hours
- **Cost:** free, and no need to register first

The written advice covers four things: your current level, the recommended starting point, the expected timeline, and when you can sit the exam.

### Step 4: registration documents

| Document | Details |
|---|---|
| Registration form | Filled in online, including an emergency contact |
| Acceptance of terms | Tick box and electronic signature |
| ID | One photocopy of photo ID (for registration purposes only) |
| Under 18 | Signed by a parent or guardian |

Your information is handled as described in our [privacy policy](/privacy).

### Step 5: payment methods

| Method | Details |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | At the school, when you enrol; **we do not store card numbers** |
| Interac e-Transfer | To the details given in your confirmation; your place is confirmed once the payment arrives |

We do not accept cash.

| Option | Instalments |
|---|---|
| Single course | 50% before the start, 50% at mid-course |
| Bundle | Monthly instalments, no fees |

There is no registration fee. Our prices are in Canadian dollars, before tax; 13% HST is added at checkout. See the [prices](/prices).

Your place is secured only after payment. A verbal hold lasts 48 hours at most.

### Step 6: before classes start

Two days before classes begin, you will receive:

1. **Resource library account** (course notes, question bank, mock exams)
2. **Class group invitation** (your classmates and teacher)
3. **First-class notice** (time, location or meeting link, what to bring)

## When classes start

A new group starts every month. A group opens as soon as 4 students have enrolled (8 at most). Class times: weekdays 18:30–21:00, Saturdays 10:00–17:00. One-on-one lessons can start at any time; teen and preschool classes run by school term (September / January / April).

**Joining a level in progress:** class hours are fixed for each level. If you join a level that has already started, you pay for the remaining hours.

## Three common sticking points

**"The test placed me one level lower than I expected"**
This is common. People usually rate themselves half a level to one level too high. Going by the test result is faster than going by expectations.

**"The class I wanted is full"**
Wait for the next group (usually a month), or start with one-on-one lessons in the meantime. We do not raise the class size limit to fill a group.

**"Can I change my start date after paying?"**
More than 7 days before the start, you can move to the next intake once for free. Within 7 days, $70 is deducted.

## Cancellation, refunds and rescheduling

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Cancel more than 7 days before start | Full refund, no fee |
| Cancel within the last 7 days before start | $70 withheld |
| Cancel after classes begin | Refund for the hours not attended, minus a $70 fee |
| One third of the course already attended | No refund |
| Cooling-off: within 10 days of signing, with no class attended | Full refund, no conditions |
| Reschedule (more than 7 days before start) | Free, once |
| Reschedule (within 7 days of start) | $70 deducted |

If you leave a bundle part-way, the hours attended are recalculated at the single-course price and the bundle discount is not refunded. All the rules are on the [prices](/prices) page and in the [terms](/terms).
