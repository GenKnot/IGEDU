Our teachers are native French speakers or hold DALF C1 or above, and each one goes through two rounds of trial lessons before teaching.

Below is what you can know about each of them: who teaches, which level, and for how many years.

## Our teaching standards

- **Entry bar**: native French speaker or DALF C1 and above, and at least 2 years of experience teaching French as a foreign language.
- **Two rounds of trial lessons**: the first is a 20-minute demonstration; in the second, the teacher gives a full lesson to real students, scored by current teachers.
- **Unified materials**: one handout set per level. Teachers may add to it, not cut from it.
- **Anonymous questionnaire**: at the end of every term, students rate their teacher anonymously.

## FAQ

**Can I choose my teacher?**
Yes. Tell us before the group opens. If that teacher is full for the term, we will offer another teacher at the same level and explain why.

**What if I want to change teacher part-way?**
It does not affect your progress. All groups at the same level share the same handouts and schedule, and your assessment report is passed on to the new teacher. You can also ask once, free of charge, to move to another group at the same level (see the [terms](/terms)).
