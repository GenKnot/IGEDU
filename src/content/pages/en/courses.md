We do not sell our courses as "packages".

Before you enrol, you take a placement test. Based on your current level and your goal (an immigration exam, studies, work, or simply using French), we build a path. Each stage of the path matches one of the courses below. You always know which stage you are at, what comes next and how long is left.

## How to choose

- **You need a score** → exam preparation
- **You are starting from zero** → level courses
- **You have the basics but one skill holds you back** → targeted skills courses
- **You are looking for a course for your child or company** → teens and corporate

## Exam preparation

For people who need a language score. All courses follow the real exam format and scoring criteria, and mock exams are scored section by section.

| Course | Exam | Target | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| TEF Canada intensive | TEF Canada | B2 / CLB (NCLC) 7 | 40 |
| TCF Canada intensive | TCF Canada | CLB (NCLC) 5-7 | 40 |
| TEFAQ / TCFQ | Quebec immigration | B1-B2 | 36 |
| DELF B2 preparation | DELF B2 | B2 | 48 |

A note: TEF Canada and TCF Canada are the language tests accepted for Canadian immigration. DELF and DALF are diplomas and do not earn Express Entry points. Before choosing a course, make sure which one you need: we will ask you this first.

See also [Exam preparation](/exam-prep).

## Level courses (A1-B2)

From the very first class, everything is in French. You do not need to know French already; you only need to be willing to speak in class.

| Level | Starting point | By the end | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Complete beginner | Everyday greetings, introducing yourself, simple questions and answers | 60 |
| A2 | Has learned the basics | Communicating in everyday situations, reading and writing short texts | 60 |
| B1 | Can manage daily life | Giving opinions, handling work and daily-life situations | 72 |
| B2 | Can communicate fairly fluently | Academic and workplace language, ready for intensive exam preparation | 72 |

See also [Levels](/levels).

## Class formats

- **Small group:** 4 to 8 students, a fixed teacher, the same classmates. A group opens once 4 students have enrolled, and a new group starts every month. Schedule: weekdays 18:30 to 21:00 and Saturdays 10:00 to 17:00. One class hour = 60 minutes.
- **One-on-one:** a schedule built around your goal, for people with irregular schedules or special progress.
- **Live online:** the same teacher, the same materials and the same speaking time as in the classroom.

## Hours and prices

Course hours are fixed per level; if you join mid-level, you pay for the remaining hours.

Small group: **$35 per hour**; business and interview French in a group: $49 per hour. One-on-one: $98 per hour. Children, teens and corporate: by term or tailored. Prices are before tax: 13% HST is added at checkout. Online and in-person classes cost the same.

Total hours, total cost and refund rules are confirmed in writing before you enrol.
