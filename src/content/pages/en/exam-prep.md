Lesson-by-lesson syllabi for our exam preparation courses. One lesson hour = 60 minutes. To choose the exam that fits your goal, see the [Exams](/exams) page.

## What applies to every course

- **All mock exams are timed**, run to the length and marking criteria of the real exam.
- **Writing and speaking are scored item by item** using the official scoring grid, not "that sounded good".
- **No guessing of exam topics.** We teach the language skill behind each task type, so a change of topic does not affect your performance.
- **Materials**: official collection of task types plus our own course notes, matched to the level ($84).

## TEFAQ / TCFQ

**36 hours · 7 modules**
**Goal**: immigration to Quebec, B1 to B2

**Structure**: for both TEFAQ and TCFQ, only **listening and speaking are compulsory**; reading and writing are optional. So 60% of the hours in this course go to speaking, and reading and writing modules are added as needed.

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **M1 Know the exam** | 5 | Differences between the two Quebec exams, compulsory and optional parts, how results are used; entry mock exam (listening + speaking) |
| **M2 Listening (CO)** | 6 | Short situations, announcements and notices, interviews; recognizing Quebec accents and local expressions |
| **M3 Speaking A** | 6 | Obtaining information: asking questions and responding in situations such as asking directions, renting a home, dealing with an office, making a complaint |
| **M4 Speaking B** | 6 | Persuading and giving an opinion: structure, connectors, positions on common topics |
| **M5 Quebec language** | 5 | Local vocabulary and expressions, getting used to the accent, cultural context (useful in interviews and in everyday errands) |
| **M6 Optional add-on (CE/EE)** | 4 | For students who need reading and writing results: task types, templates, timed practice |
| **M7 Mock exams and final push** | 4 | Two full mock oral exams with review; pre-exam checklist |

| Module | Hours | Content |

## Single-skill reinforcement

**20 hours · 4 modules**
**Who it is for**: students who have finished an intensive course and are missing only one skill. You choose one of CO, CE, EE or EO.
**What it solves**: the case of "total score is enough, but one part is not".

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Task types and baseline | 5 | Overview of the task types and scoring criteria for that skill; one past exam set to establish your baseline |
| M2 Targeted errors | 5 | Sorting your errors by category and working through each one |
| M3 Timed practice | 5 | Three timed sessions, question-by-question review, building an answering rhythm |
| M4 Full mock exam | 5 | A full mock exam for that skill, score forecast and pre-exam checklist |
