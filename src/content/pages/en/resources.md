Class hours are only time: what you learn depends on what fills that time.

Each of our courses comes with three things: handouts, an exercise bank and mock exams. One student account opens all three, valid for one year. The textbook (printed handouts plus the online exercise bank for one year) costs $84 per level before tax and is included in every bundle (see [Prices](/prices)).

## Course handouts

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Basis | Written from CECRL (CEFR) descriptors; each level has clear competence goals |
| Structure | Each module includes: a grammar point, communication tasks, a vocabulary theme, after-class exercises and a progress assessment |
| Format | Printed handouts (given out in class) plus an electronic version (downloadable from your account) |
| Consistency | One set per level: teachers may add, never remove |
| Updates | Revised once a year |

"May add, never remove" is not a slogan: it ensures that if you change teacher or class, you do not miss anything you are supposed to learn.

## Mock exams

- Real time limits, with no pause during the test
- Results the same day: your four scores and a breakdown of where you lost points
- A dedicated mock-exam room, separate from the teaching area; the listening section is played once; one invigilator per session
- The speaking part is taken at the school (or by submitting an online recording)
- Price: $42 per mock exam; enrolled students get one free per month

See the [Mock exams](/mock-exams) page.

## Listening and reading material

Organized by level, not a random selection of recordings:

| Level | Type of material |
|---|---|
| A1–A2 | Slow dialogues, everyday situations, short announcements |
| B1 | Radio news, interview extracts, everyday-life podcasts |
| B2 | Full news reports, debate extracts, lectures |
| C1 | Unabridged lectures, academic interviews |

**The biggest trap in French listening is textbook audio that is "too standard".** At real speed there are liaisons, elisions and accents. They need to be in the material so you are not caught off guard on exam day.

## Writing models and corrected samples

- For each level, 10 model texts, marked to show where points are won and lost
- 3 "weak, average, good" versions of the same topic: three ways of writing the same answer
- Your corrected texts are archived so you can look back at your progress
