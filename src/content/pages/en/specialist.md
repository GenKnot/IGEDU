Syllabi for specialist courses, youth courses, adult courses, corporate courses and one-on-one lessons. One lesson hour = 60 minutes. For how combined classes are put together, see the [catalogue](/catalogue).

## Speaking and listening

**24 hours · 6 modules**
**Who it is for**: you understand but cannot speak; you have a strong accent; you freeze in the exam speaking test; you work in Canada but cannot get a word in during meetings.

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Understanding real speed | 4 | Liaison and elision, getting used to the speed, catching key words rather than every word |
| M2 The first step to speaking | 4 | Keeping going for 30 seconds with simple sentences; working around a word you do not know |
| M3 Structure and linking | 4 | Joining short sentences into a paragraph with connectors; avoiding "and then… and then…" |
| M4 Real-life situations | 4 | Job interview, meeting, doctor's visit, talking with your child's teacher |
| M5 Pronunciation and intonation | 4 | Nasal vowels, voiced and unvoiced consonants, intonation at the end of a sentence (question vs. statement) |
| M6 Practice and review | 4 | Pair practice, recording and item-by-item feedback |

## Listening

**20 hours · 4 modules**
**Who it is for**: you cannot keep up with the speed, you cannot hear liaison, or you understand but do not remember.

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Sound level | 5 | Liaison, elision, nasal vowels: often you do not understand because you cannot hear where words begin and end |
| M2 Word-chunk level | 5 | Frequent collocations and fixed expressions, so you can guess the whole after hearing half |
| M3 Text level | 5 | Catching structure and logic words instead of translating word by word |
| M4 Real practice | 5 | Close listening to four kinds of material: news, interviews, voicemail messages, announcements |

## Writing correction workshop

**20 hours · 5 modules**
**Who it is for**: disorganized structure, grammar errors, difficulty reaching the word count, always a few points short in the exam writing test.

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Scoring criteria | 4 | How task completion, coherence, vocabulary and grammar are scored |
| M2 Sentence level | 4 | Tense consistency, gender and number agreement, avoiding calques from your first language |
| M3 Paragraph level | 4 | Topic sentence, evidence, concession, conclusion |
| M4 Text level | 4 | Connectors by level, register, controlling length |
| M5 Timed practice | 4 | 4 timed writing tasks, sentence-by-sentence correction and rewriting |

**What sets it apart**: writing is the one skill that only improves through correction. Every piece is annotated sentence by sentence, and you must rewrite it after the corrections.

## Pronunciation and phonetics

**20 hours · 5 modules**
**Who it is for**: all levels. You speak slowly, cannot understand your own speech, or are asked by native speakers to repeat yourself.

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Vowel system | 4 | The 16 vowels, with a focus on the ones that are hardest to hear and produce |
| M2 Nasal vowels | 4 | an / on / in / un, including regional differences |
| M3 Consonants and voicing | 4 | Aspiration of p/b, t/d, k/g; the French r |
| M4 Liaison and fluency | 4 | Liaison, elision, rhythm; why French sounds "flat" |
| M5 Intonation and tone | 4 | Questions, surprise, displeasure, politeness: it is all in the intonation |

## Vocabulary and grammar refinement

**24 hours · 6 modules**
**Who it is for**: you have studied for years and still make the same mistakes; you understand the grammar book but cannot use it.

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Articles, gender and number | 4 | The part of French that causes errors for longest |
| M2 Verb tense system | 4 | The three past tenses, future and conditional, sorted out in one go |
| M3 Pronoun system | 4 | en / y / direct and indirect objects / relative pronouns |
| M4 Prepositions and collocations | 4 | Verb + preposition combinations, learned as word chunks rather than rules |
| M5 Subjunctive and moods | 4 | When it is required, when it must not be used |
| M6 Frequent word chunks | 4 | 200 frequent collocations, ready to use in writing and speaking |

## Interview French

**20 hours · 4 modules**
**Who it is for**: job interviews and the Quebec selection interview for the CSQ. **Rate: $49 per hour (group).**

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Introducing yourself | 5 | A 90-second version and a 3-minute version; structure matters more than fluency |
| M2 Frequent questions | 5 | Why Canada, career plans, strengths and weaknesses, reason for leaving a job |

## Preschool French

**32 hours per term**
**Who it is for**: children aged 3 to 5. **Schedule**: 2 hours a week for 16 weeks.

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1–M2 | 8 | Greetings, colours, numbers 1 to 10, parts of the body (songs and movements) |
| M3–M4 | 8 | Animals, food, family; understanding simple instructions |
| M5–M6 | 8 | Everyday phrases and polite expressions; describing pictures in French |
| M7–M8 | 8 | Short stories and role play; end-of-term showcase for parents |

**Note**: at this age we do not teach grammar and we do not correct pronunciation mistakes. The goal is not to be afraid of French.

## Youth French

**32 hours per term**
**Who it is for**: children aged 6 to 12 who are in, or about to enter, a French immersion class. **Schedule**: 2 hours a week for 16 weeks.

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1–M2 | 8 | Classroom French: understanding the teacher's instructions, asking questions, asking for help |
| M3–M4 | 8 | Reading and spelling: where students in immersion most often fall behind |
| M5–M6 | 8 | Speaking and presenting: daring to speak French in front of the class |
| M7–M8 | 8 | Introduction to grammar and short written sentences; end-of-term showcase |

**Communication with parents**: every 4 weeks, a short written report on what the child practised over those four weeks and where they are stuck.

## IB / AP French

**32 hours per term**
**Who it is for**: high school students preparing for IB French B or AP French. **Schedule**: 2 hours a week for 16 weeks.

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | 8 | Exam structure and scoring; scoring points of the individual oral (IB) or the conversation (AP) |
| M2 | 8 | Writing by text type: the format of letters, articles and reviews |
| M3 | 8 | Thematic vocabulary: identities, experiences, human ingenuity, social organization |
| M4 | 8 | Mock assessment and item-by-item review |

## Settling-in French for newcomers

**20 hours · 5 modules**
**Who it is for**: newcomers who have just arrived or are about to. **No grammar exam: we only deal with the situations you will need in the next few days.**

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Errands | 4 | Getting documents, opening an account, the post office, Service Canada vocabulary |
| M2 Housing | 4 | Viewing a home, signing a lease, talking with the landlord, requesting repairs |
| M3 Health care | 4 | Making an appointment, describing symptoms, reading medicine box instructions, what to say in an emergency |
| M4 Children and school | 4 | Meeting the teacher, parent meetings, reading school notices |
| M5 Daily life | 4 | Supermarket, bus, neighbours, emergencies |
