For a specific question, call or write to us. We usually reply within 1 business day.

If you are not sure which level to start at, or you would like to see the campus first, just make an appointment: no need to enrol beforehand.

## Visiting us


**Please book a time first:** classrooms are in use during classes, and if you arrive unannounced you may have to wait in the lobby. Let us know, and someone will show you around. See also the [Campus](/campus) page.
