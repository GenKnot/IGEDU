The DELF is a diploma: once you pass, it is valid for life. This path does not earn immigration points; it is for study and professional qualifications.

### Who it is for

- You plan to apply to a program taught in French, or you need a B2 diploma for professional certification.
- You want proof of language level that never expires.
- You are not in a hurry and are happy to progress step by step over 12 months.

### Who it is not for

- If you want immigration points, the DELF does not give them: take the TEF / TCF route.
- If you need a diploma within three months, that isn't possible.

### Stages

| Stage | Timing | Content | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Months 1–4 | A1.1 → A2.2 | 120 |
| 2 | Months 5–8 | B1.1 + B1.2 | 72 |
| 3 | Months 9–12 | DELF B2 preparation | 48 |

### Included

240 hours, textbooks, placement test, free Q&A for 3 months after you finish.

### Good to know

The DELF and DALF are diplomas and do not earn points for Canadian immigration. TEF Canada and TCF Canada are the language tests recognized for immigration.
