Three terms and a summer camp. What parents fear most is not slow progress, but a child who forgets everything over the summer.

### Who it is for

- Children aged 6–12 enrolled in French Immersion.
- They keep up in class, but their reading and writing are clearly behind.
- They need ongoing support rather than last-minute cramming.

### Made up of

| Part | Timing | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Fall term | September to December | 32 |
| Winter term | January to April | 32 |
| Spring term | April to June | 32 |
| Summer camp | July, 4 weeks | 40 |

### Communication with parents

Written feedback every 4 weeks, and a 20-minute meeting each term. The feedback covers only three things: what was practised over these four weeks, where your child is stuck, and how to practise at home.
