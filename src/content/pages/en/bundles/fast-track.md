You already have B1. What you are missing is the score and a final push in speaking.

### Who it is for

- You have a B1 foundation (2–3 years of study, or a B1 course completed).
- Your goal is CLB 7 (NCLC 7) on TEF Canada, with an application due within a year.
- You are worried about the interview or speaking test and want to rehearse it beforehand.

### Who it is not for

- If you are starting from zero, look at the complete path.
- If you only want to drill practice tests without working on the language itself: a large part of this course goes to building language foundations.

### Included and not included

**Included:** 204 hours, textbooks, placement test, three full mock exams, interview simulation (video-recorded, with feedback point by point), help registering for the exam and accompaniment to a partner test centre, free Q&A for 3 months after you finish.
**Not included:** exam registration fees, extra one-on-one lessons.

### FAQ

**How is my B1 level established?**
You take a free 40-minute placement test, which includes a speaking part. If the result is below B1, we will advise you to take B1.2 first rather than force you into the class.

**Is French for interviews required?**
It is required for CSQ applicants. For fast-track-only applicants, it can be replaced by 20 hours of single-skill exam booster.
