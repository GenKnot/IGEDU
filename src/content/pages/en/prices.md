## At a glance

Small-group classes are **$35 per hour**. One hour of class is 60 minutes, on site or online, at the same price. One-on-one lessons are $98 per hour; business French and interview French in a group are $49 per hour.

**All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and before tax: 13% HST is added at checkout.** We accept credit card and Interac e-Transfer, not cash. The placement test is free and there is no registration fee.

Bundles cost less than the courses they contain when bought separately. To estimate your own total, try the [cost calculator](/cost-calculator).

## What is included and what is not

**Included in tuition**

- All class hours
- An account for the resource library (handouts, exercise bank, mock-exam material), valid for one year
- Progress assessments and a written report

**Included in bundles only**

- The textbook
- Free Q&A sessions for 3 months after the course ends

**Not included**

- The textbook (except in bundles)
- Official exam registration fees, paid to the organization that runs the exam
- Extra one-on-one hours
- Retake and resit fees

## Refunds

- More than 7 days before the course starts: full refund, no fee.
- Within the last 7 days before the course starts: $70 is withheld.
- After the course starts: refund for the hours not attended, minus a $70 fee.
- **No refund once one third of the course has been attended.**
- Cooling-off period: within 10 days of signing, if you have not attended any class, you can cancel for a full refund, no conditions.
- Bundles: if you leave part-way, the hours attended are recalculated at the single-course price and the bundle discount is not refunded.
- Absence: tell us at least 24 hours in advance to get one make-up lesson; otherwise the class counts as attended.
- Transfer to another group at the same level: once, free of charge.

See the [terms](/terms) (version 1.0) for the full text.

## Two notes on our prices

**Why there is no "regular price, sale price"**
Our price is our price. No limited-time discounts, and no raise followed by a markdown.

**Why bundles are priced as pathways, not as hourly discounts**
The bundle price assumes you complete the whole pathway. If you leave early, the hours attended are counted at the single-course price. We state it here, not only at enrolment.
