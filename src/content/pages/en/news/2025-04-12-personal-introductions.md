## Making basic structures personal

This beginner class focused closely on introducing yourself. Name, age, languages and interests supplied the content for short exchanges, while grammar was introduced through use. Linking a structure to a personal detail gave learners a concrete reason to choose a word, arrange a sentence and say it aloud.

## Putting the language into practice

The group of 17 offered a smaller setting for guided oral practice. Rather than assembling a long biography, the work concentrated on clear, manageable statements. That is a useful foundation for later conversation: a brief introduction that can be adapted is easier to reuse than a paragraph learned word for word.

## A practice idea to take further

Try Je parle anglais et j’apprends le français. Change the languages to match your experience. Say the sentence naturally before adding an age, occupation or hobby.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
