## Putting the language into practice

The session also introduced the idea of continuing with structured study. A first experience can reveal both useful starting points and areas that need more time, such as pronunciation or understanding replies. Connecting the activities offered learners an overview of how basic skills can be developed together in subsequent lessons.

## A practice idea to take further

Choose two everyday scenes and act them out without a full script. Keep a small list of useful phrases nearby. Afterwards, note which question or reply you would like to practise in your next lesson.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
