## Putting the language into practice

Group practice was central to the session. It asked participants to use the structures with another person and attend to a reply. At beginner level, a brief conversation about music, sport or reading can provide valuable practice in turn-taking: answering a question, adding a detail and giving the other person space to respond.

## A practice idea to take further

Start with J’aime lire. Add one detail, such as J’aime les romans, then ask Et vous ? Aim for an exchange with two speakers rather than a long speech about yourself.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
