## Putting the language into practice

Simulated exchanges allowed participants to bring those phrases into conversation. Preparing for a trip involves both speaking and recognizing a reply: a departure time, a booking confirmation or a question about an order. The session connected these needs through practical scenarios that could be revisited before departure.

## A practice idea to take further

Prepare three short exchanges, one for each setting. Include a request and a clarification phrase such as Plus lentement, s’il vous plaît. Review the numbers you might hear in a time, room number or price.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
