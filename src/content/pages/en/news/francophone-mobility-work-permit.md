Francophone Mobility can allow an eligible employer to hire a French-speaking worker outside Quebec without obtaining a Labour Market Impact Assessment. The exemption simplifies one part of the process; it does not remove the need to qualify for a work permit.

> **Keep three checks separate:** the worker’s French ability, the qualifying job offer outside Quebec, and the remaining work-permit requirements.

## The language requirement is specific

IRCC requires evidence of intermediate French speaking and listening, equivalent to NCLC 5 or higher. This differs from the four-skill NCLC 7 requirement of the Express Entry French-language category. Reaching one threshold does not automatically establish eligibility for the other measure.

Check IRCC’s accepted ways of documenting ability before booking a test solely for this purpose. The evidence required for a work-permit application is not necessarily identical to that required for Express Entry.

## The job and employer still matter

The offer must involve work outside Quebec. IRCC permits offers across TEER categories, except primary agriculture occupations in TEER 4 and 5. Other eligibility and admissibility conditions still apply.

The employer also has a role in the process, including the applicable employer-compliance steps. “LMIA-exempt” does not mean that a worker can create an offer independently, disregard employer obligations or start work before authorization.

| Common assumption | More accurate interpretation |
|---|---|
| French alone secures the permit | Language is one condition among several. |
| No LMIA means no employer process | Employer obligations still apply. |
| A permit guarantees permanent residence | Permanent residence requires a separate eligible pathway. |

## Plan beyond the application

Language preparation should include the communication the job will require: understanding instructions, clarifying a problem and participating in workplace conversations. Passing a threshold is useful, but day-to-day confidence develops through wider practice.

If permanent residence is also a goal, examine that possibility separately. Whether particular work experience counts depends on the later program’s criteria. Obtain advice on the actual job and permit conditions rather than assuming that temporary employment automatically creates an immigration pathway.
