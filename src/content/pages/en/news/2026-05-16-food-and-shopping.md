## Putting the language into practice

With 16 participants, the class provided a smaller setting for scenario work. Comparing the two themes made the reusable parts of an exchange easier to identify. A greeting and polite request remain useful whether the next word names a drink, a food item or something needed from a shop.

## A practice idea to take further

Make a short shopping list and a short café order. Use Je voudrais… for both, then add a quantity where appropriate. Check that your request includes the item and enough information for the other person to respond.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
