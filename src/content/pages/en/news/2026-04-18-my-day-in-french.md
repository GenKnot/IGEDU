## Putting the language into practice

A personal routine gives a beginner an accessible sequence to work with: what happens first, what follows and when. The class connected vocabulary to that order, making it possible to build a short account without describing every detail. It also created a reason to ask someone else about their day.

## A practice idea to take further

Write three sentences beginning Le matin, À midi and Le soir. Include one activity in each. Read the sequence aloud, then ask a partner Et vous, qu’est-ce que vous faites le matin ?

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
