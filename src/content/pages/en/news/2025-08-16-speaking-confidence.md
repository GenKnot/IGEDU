## A manageable beginning, middle and end

This open class addressed a familiar obstacle: hesitating to speak even when a few expressions are already known. Conversation openings, answers to common questions and polite ways to finish an exchange gave participants a manageable structure. The focus was on using existing knowledge rather than waiting until every possible sentence felt secure.

## Putting the language into practice

Participants practised completing short conversations. Confidence in this setting was linked to an observable task: begin an exchange, contribute something and bring it to a close. Repeated attempts can make that sequence more familiar, while still leaving room for pauses, clarification and further language learning.

## A practice idea to take further

Prepare one opening question and one closing phrase, such as Qu’est-ce que vous aimez faire ? and Merci, à bientôt. Practise a brief exchange rather than testing how long you can keep speaking.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
