## Greetings, family and the weather

The autumn introductory class centred on familiar subjects: greeting someone, introducing family members and talking about the weather. These topics gave newcomers a way to connect new sounds with information they already knew. Teacher demonstrations and repeated speaking practice provided a framework for trying the expressions aloud.

## Putting the language into practice

Using everyday topics also kept the learning task specific. A learner could focus on one relationship, one greeting or one description of the weather, then reuse the structure with a different word. This is a practical introduction to building sentences gradually, without needing to describe an entire situation at once.

## A practice idea to take further

Try Voici ma sœur or Voici mon frère, then a short weather phrase such as Il fait frais. Choose examples that reflect your own life, and check the pronunciation of the words you change.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
