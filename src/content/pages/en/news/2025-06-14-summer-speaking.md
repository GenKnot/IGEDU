## Clear intentions in familiar situations

Travel, shopping and social situations supplied the material for this summer speaking session. The class focused on expressions with an immediate purpose: asking for something, sharing a preference or beginning an exchange. Clear pronunciation mattered, while the practical aim was to make the intended message understandable and give conversation a starting point.

## Putting the language into practice

Simulated situations allowed learners to try those expressions in context. The session emphasized participation without requiring every sentence to be perfect at the first attempt. This approach gives beginners something specific to work on: make the request, listen to the reply and adjust the wording when needed.

## A practice idea to take further

Choose one situation you expect this summer and prepare three phrases for it. Include a greeting, a request and a way to ask for repetition. Practise them as a short exchange.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
