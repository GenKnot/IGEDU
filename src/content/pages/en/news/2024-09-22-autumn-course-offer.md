## A starting point that fits your goals

Interest is a useful beginning, but regular practice needs a place in the calendar. A learner might want to understand everyday questions, take part in a short conversation or prepare for future study. Naming that purpose helps narrow the course choice and makes it easier to recognize what needs attention during practice.

## Making room for steady practice

The campaign encouraged enquiries about course content and schedules so that learners could compare their plans with the time available. A workable rhythm includes both attendance and the smaller tasks around it: revisiting a conversation, listening again to a phrase and bringing a question to the next lesson.
