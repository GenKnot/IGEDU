## Connecting the first sounds with a useful task

The spring introductory class began with the alphabet and basic sound patterns before moving into café and restaurant situations. This progression connected the written language with expressions that could be spoken for a purpose. Listening, repetition, questions and role play offered different ways to approach the same beginner material.

## Putting the language into practice

The practical situations gave pronunciation a clear destination. A word introduced in an early exercise could return inside a request, followed by a reply from another person. The session therefore offered a first view of how several skills work together, rather than treating listening and speaking as separate stages.

## A practice idea to take further

Choose one café phrase and identify the words you can hear in it. Repeat it as a whole, then use it in a two-person exchange. Return to the audio if a sound remains unclear.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
