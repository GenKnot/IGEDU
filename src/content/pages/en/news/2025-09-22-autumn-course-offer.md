## A starting point that fits your goals

Travel, personal interest and future study can lead to different learning priorities. Everyday situations offer concrete material in each case, but a suitable course also needs to match the learner’s current understanding. A person who recognizes many written words may still need guided listening and speaking practice before using them comfortably in conversation.
