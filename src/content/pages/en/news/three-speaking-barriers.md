Not speaking can have different causes. You may know what you want to say but fear making a mistake, have language available but no idea to develop, or need too long to retrieve familiar words.

> **Identify the barrier before choosing the exercise. Confidence, ideas and retrieval need related but different kinds of practice.**

## When fear stops the first sentence

Start with a low-pressure exchange about familiar information. Agree with a partner that corrections will come after the short conversation, rather than interrupting every sentence. Then select one recurring issue to review.

The aim is not to ignore accuracy. It is to create enough uninterrupted communication to discover what needs work. A supportive routine gives mistakes a practical role instead of treating them as a verdict on ability.

## When you have little to say

Prepare ideas, not memorized speeches. For a common topic, note a preference, a reason, an example and a question you could ask someone else. Use experiences you can explain honestly with your current language.

If the topic is unfamiliar, begin by clarifying it. Asking a relevant question is part of communication, not a failure to deliver an instant opinion.

## When words arrive slowly

Practise retrieving useful short expressions without reading. For example: *À mon avis…*, *Ce que je veux dire…*, or *Pouvez-vous préciser… ?* Then use each expression in a new exchange.

| Barrier | Small exercise |
|---|---|
| Fear of errors | One uninterrupted minute, followed by focused feedback. |
| Few ideas | One reason, one example and one follow-up question. |
| Slow retrieval | Recall a useful expression and adapt it immediately. |

Do not impose an arbitrary three-second deadline on every answer. Natural speech includes pauses, and speed alone does not establish fluency. Record a short conversation occasionally and compare clarity, responsiveness and independence over time. Progress often appears first as a conversation you can continue, rather than a speech delivered without hesitation.
