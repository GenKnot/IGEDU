## Putting the language into practice

Short exchanges linked Bonjour with a follow-up question and response. That small extension changes the task: the speaker must listen and leave room for someone else. For beginners, a brief greeting sequence can provide a useful introduction to the social choices that accompany vocabulary and pronunciation.

## A practice idea to take further

Compare Comment allez-vous ? and Comment vas-tu ? Practise each in a suitable imagined setting, then add a reply and a return question. Keep the level of familiarity consistent through the exchange.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
