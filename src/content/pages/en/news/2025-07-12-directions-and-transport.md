## Putting the language into practice

The theme also brought listening into the foreground. A successful request for directions depends on recognizing enough of the answer to take the next step. Pairing an oral instruction with a visible route offers a practical way to check understanding before trying the same task with fewer visual supports.

## A practice idea to take further

On a simple map, ask Où est la gare ? Have a partner use à gauche, à droite and tout droit to guide you. Trace the route as you listen, then switch roles.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
