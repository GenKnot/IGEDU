Quebec’s French-language requirements cannot be reduced to a single rule about two skills. The relevant program, applicant role and accepted form of evidence determine what you need to demonstrate.

> **Check the program first. Do not select test modules from a general claim that Quebec only requires oral French.**

## A modular test does not define the application requirement

Some Quebec-oriented test formats allow separate components. That flexibility does not mean every applicant may choose only listening and speaking. A program can require written abilities as well, and the requirements for a principal applicant and accompanying spouse may differ.

Test names also matter. TEFAQ, TCF Québec, TEF Canada and TCF Canada are not interchangeable labels, even when more than one is recognized by a particular program. Read the accepted-evidence list rather than relying on an abbreviation used in a discussion group.

## Make a requirements sheet before registering

| Information to record | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Exact program and stream | Requirements are program-specific. |
| Applicant’s role | Different family members can face different conditions. |
| Skills and required levels | Oral requirements do not establish written exemptions. |
| Accepted evidence and recency | A recognized qualification may still need recent results. |

Use Quebec’s official instructions for this sheet. If a requirement appears to have changed, compare the effective date and any transition rules before booking.

## Prepare the required skills without neglecting practical French

If your application requires oral evidence, allocate substantial time to listening and interaction. Still, reading workplace messages or writing a short explanation may matter in daily life. An administrative requirement should not become the boundary of your learning.

For a learner considering both federal and Quebec pathways, clarify the intended destination before choosing a test. The French category in Express Entry concerns settlement outside Quebec and has different conditions. A sensible test choice begins with the actual application, followed by an honest assessment of every skill it requires.
