## The language of a first meeting

The class brought together the small expressions that make a first meeting work: greetings, goodbyes, thanks and questions about time. Scenario cards encouraged learners to select language for a particular situation, rather than repeat the same sentence regardless of context. This made politeness part of the exchange from the beginning.

## Putting the language into practice

Moving between roles gave participants a chance to link expressions into short conversations. A greeting could lead to a question, an answer and a closing remark. For beginners, that structure is useful because it turns a collection of remembered words into an exchange with a beginning and an end.

## A practice idea to take further

Build an exchange with four steps: Bonjour; a question; a reply; Merci, au revoir. Change only the question on the next attempt, so that the familiar structure supports a new piece of language.

A short exercise is most useful when you return to it. Revisit the exchange on another day, change one detail and notice what you can say without checking your notes. Use any remaining hesitation to choose a question for your next lesson.
