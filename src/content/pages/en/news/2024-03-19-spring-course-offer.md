## A starting point that fits your goals

For a complete beginner, a useful first goal is modest and concrete: recognize common greetings, introduce yourself and ask a simple question. Someone returning to French may instead need to revisit pronunciation or rebuild listening habits. The campaign invited both groups to discuss a suitable starting point rather than choose a course on price alone.

## Making room for steady practice

A course becomes easier to follow when there is room for practice between lessons. Choosing a realistic weekly slot, keeping a few useful phrases at hand and returning to them aloud can turn a seasonal intention into an ongoing routine. That planning remains relevant after the offer itself has ended.
