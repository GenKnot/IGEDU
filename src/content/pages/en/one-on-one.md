One-on-one is not a "premium version" of a small-group class.

Its value is this: you arrive with a specific problem, and the teacher works only on that. It might be speaking stuck at B1, writing that is always three points short, or an exam in two months with no time for a step-by-step programme.

## Who it suits

- **Fixing a weak skill:** your other three skills are fine, one is dragging your score down
- **Irregular schedule:** shift work, business travel, young children, so no fixed timetable is possible
- **Unusual progress:** you studied before but stopped for many years, and need to find your gaps quickly
- **Single goal:** take the TEF within two months, practising only for the exam
- **Hard to join a group:** speaking anxiety, need for a separate setting

## Who it does not suit

- **Complete beginners:** they most need conversation with peers and a fixed rhythm; one-on-one is actually slower
- **Anyone who just wants someone to chat with:** a French conversation club is a better fit than paid one-on-one
- **Anyone trying to save money:** for the same hours, the small-group rate ($35 per hour) is about one third

**Our advice:** if you are starting from zero, begin with a small group at A1.1 to build the habit of speaking, then add one-on-one lessons for a specific weakness. This combination costs less than going fully one-on-one, and works better.

## How it works

| Stage | What happens | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | Free placement test, then clarifying your goal and deadline | 40 minutes |
| Plan | Written study plan: how many hours, what to practise each time, when you will be assessed | Delivered within 3 days of the test |
| Lessons | Following the plan, each lesson has a clear outcome | 60 minutes per lesson hour |
| Review | Every 8 hours, to check the stage goal has been met | 30 minutes |

**The written plan is not optional.** What is most easily wasted in one-on-one lessons is deciding on the spot, each time, what to cover.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I choose my teacher?**
Yes, by specialty (exams, pronunciation, business, young learners). Once chosen, the teacher does not change during that period. To meet our teachers, see the [Team](/team) page.

**Can I buy just 3 lessons?**
Yes, at $98 per hour. But under 5 lessons it is hard to build a rhythm; we recommend at least 10.

**Can unused hours be refunded?**
Refunds follow the policy set out in our [terms](/terms) and on the [Prices](/prices) page. The textbook fee is not refunded.

**Is online the same price as in person?**
Yes. Same teacher, same materials.
