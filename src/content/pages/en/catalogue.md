## Layer 2: bundles

A bundle is not a new course. It combines the single courses above into a complete path toward one goal, at a price below the total of the individual courses. The textbook is included in every bundle, and the price can be paid in monthly instalments with no fee. Details for each bundle (who it is for, stages, what is included, FAQ) are on the [Bundles](/bundles) page.

## How long does it take?

Going from zero to B2 takes about 500 hours of effective study. Our complete path is 330 class hours plus self-study, about 14 months; the full-time option is 300 class hours over 12 weeks. You know before you enroll how long the path will take.
